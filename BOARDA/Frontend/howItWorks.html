How Boarda Works

For Students:

  1. Create an account — Sign up as a student in under a minute
  2. Search listings — Filter by location, price, and amenities
  3. Compare options — View photos, details, and landlord contact info
  4. Reach out & move in — Contact the landlord directly to arrange viewing and payment

Register as a landlord

  1. List your property — Add photos, price, location, and amenities
  2. Get discovered — Students searching your area see your listing
  3. Connect with tenants — Respond to inquiries directly

FAQs

  • Is Boarda free to use?" — Yes, searching is free for students
  • Does Boarda handle payments?" — No, payments are arranged directly between student and landlord
