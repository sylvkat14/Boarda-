Making Student Accommodation Easier

Boarda is a student accommodation platform designed to make it easier for students to find suitable boarding houses. We connect students with boarding-house providers and give them a simple way to search, compare, and choose accommodation that meets their needs.
Instead of spending hours visiting different boarding houses or depending only on word of mouth, students can use Boarda to explore available accommodation in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding student accommodation simple, convenient, and accessible.
We aim to help students find accommodation that fits their budget, preferred location, and lifestyle while helping landlords reach students looking for a place to stay.

Our Vision

A Better Way to Find Student Accommodation.
Our vision is to become a trusted platform for student accommodation, helping students find safe, affordable, and convenient places to call home.

What We Do?

Boarda provides a platform where students can:

  • Search for boarding houses by location.
  • Compare different accommodation options.
  • Check prices and available facilities.
  • View boarding-house details and photos.
  • Save preferred listings.
  • Contact landlords directly.
  • Find accommodation that suits their needs.

Landlords can also use BODA to:

  • Create boarding-house listings.
  • Provide information about their properties.
  • Reach students looking for accommodation.
  • Manage their listings.
  • Communicate with interested students.

Why BODA Was Created?

Finding student accommodation can sometimes be difficult and time-consuming.
Students may have to visit different areas, make several phone calls, or depend on information from friends and other students. BODA was created to provide a simpler solution by bringing accommodation information together in one platform.
